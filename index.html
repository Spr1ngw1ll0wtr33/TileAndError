    How to play

    Tap a free tile to move it into the tray. A tile is free when nothing lies on top of it and its left or right side is clear.

    When two matching tiles meet in the tray, they vanish. Any flower matches any flower, and any season matches any season.

    The tray holds four. If it fills with no pair, you can restart the level or carry on: the tray tiles go back on the board, at a cost of four reverse moves (or as many as you have).

    Clear the board to finish the level. Every level can be cleared.

    Helps

    • Hint lights up a good move.
    • Shuffle re-deals the tiles left on the board.
    • Reverse move takes back your last tap.
    • Hammer smashes any tile, even a covered one, along with a matching tile.

    You start with one of each. Every 10 levels you choose a prize; every 50, choose three; every 100, you get one of each.
